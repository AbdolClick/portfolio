About us

Ten years of
getting up at 4am.

Tom started baking in a rented flat in Southville, mostly because he couldn't find a loaf he liked. Two years and a lot of flat, sad bread later, he and Inês took over the old butcher's on North Street.

There are six of us now. Whatever's left at closing goes to the food bank on East Street, and our starter, Doris, is older than the shop.
