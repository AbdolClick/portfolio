The atelier

A small salon,
on purpose.

Elena opened Noir in 2013 with three chairs and one rule: nobody gets rushed. We've grown to five chairs and that's where we're stopping, so we're rarely running late and never double-booked.

We colour with Davines and use Olaplex on every lightening service. And if the look you've saved on Instagram won't work on your hair, we'll tell you, then find one that will.
